About AI Reels Studio

AI Reels Studio is a tool designed to help creators prepare and manage short-form video content.

How it works

  1. Story preparation: Create or import a story, narration and scene information.
  2. Media preparation: Generate or select scene visuals and create a voice-over.
  3. Video rendering: Combine the selected media, narration and captions into a vertical MP4.
  4. Publishing workflow: Connect supported social accounts and submit content using the permissions granted by each platform.

Social account connections

Connecting an account is optional. The application requests platform permissions to perform the actions described during authorization. Available publishing features depend on the platform, account eligibility, app review status and granted permissions.

Creator control

Users are responsible for reviewing generated material, confirming captions and settings, and ensuring that they have the rights and permissions needed to publish their content.

AI Reels Studio is an independent application. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, YouTube or their parent companies.
